Use the transferable mechanism and judgment; reject recognizable surface identity. Current explicit project facts remain authoritative for the subject. This corpus—not generic model taste—determines what counts as coherent, immersive, polished, gimmicky, feasible, and worthy of further development.

### Son Daven

- Reachability and limitations: Reachable in the Codex in-app Browser at 1280×720. Homepage measured about 28,120 px, or 39 viewports. Cookie panel was dismissed before the main visual pass.
- Pages/states inspected: Full English homepage; news index; all three discovered news articles; construction-progress index; all five discovered construction updates; full-screen menu; anchor navigation into Advantages; first expanded benefit detail. - Story/sequence map: Immersive architectural promise → mythic prologue → rational about/concept → geographic grounding → experiential benefits → investment timeline and financial proof → apartment product detail → seasonality/developer credibility/gallery → news and conversion → FAQ → emblematic animal-stack resolution.
- Composition and hierarchy: Alternates full-frame imagery, monumental centered serif statements, asymmetric two-column editorial layouts, split-tone panels, modular collages, sparse data fields, and cinematic gallery frames. Small technical sans text functions as metadata against very large classical serif headlines.
- Spacing, scale, density, and visual rhythm: Major headings receive near-empty viewports; dense commercial information is isolated into bounded scenes rather than stacked continuously. The site changes density sharply but retains alignment anchors and palette continuity. It uses silence before large statements and after busy collages.
- Typography, color, imagery/video, and art direction: Near-monochrome black/bone palette with warm natural imagery. Classical serif supplies heritage and narrative gravity; compact futuristic/technical sans supplies investment and interface detail. Architectural renders and editorial photography stay within a restrained earthy grade.
- Motion grammar and input mapping: Scroll controls mask deformation, image scale, background inversion, collage travel, and scene replacement. The vertical woven marks behave as a causal material: they assemble, dissolve, reveal, crop, and bridge scenes. Motion varies by scene while retaining this shared material law.
- Scene connections and transitions: Instead of generic fades, one scene is often eaten, woven, or extruded into the next by the recurring line motif. Full-frame architectural space can collapse into a small central window, then the surrounding motif takes over; later the same logic reveals imagery beneath text and data. Index and article pages use a simpler but related transition: pale editorial content is absorbed by a dark woven threshold before the site-wide totem footer.
- Engineered peak and resolution: The opening establishes the strongest transformation vocabulary; the late footer resolves it into a centered totem of animals with contact information balanced on both sides. The close holds as a graphic destination rather than dissolving into an ordinary footer.
- What creates polish: Strong motif reuse without identical animations; deliberate alternation between spectacle and readable commercial content; palette discipline; typography roles remain stable; motion prepares the next scene rather than merely animating the current one; layouts change topology instead of repeating a section template. Crucially, intensity changes by page mode: long articles suppress spectacle for comprehension, while construction updates use dark documentary layouts and restrained motif fragments. Both still resolve into the same animated footer world.
- What would make the same technique gimmicky: Using the line treatment as a filter on every asset without changing its narrative function; sacrificing text legibility to the motif; repeating the same mask timing; adding unrelated parallax or floating UI on top of the established material system.
- User-specific preference: Confirms the user's attraction to a site that feels simple and well designed because the motion system compresses many sections into one coherent visual language.
- Transferable principles: Derive motion from a brand material or story property; let one motif take multiple structural roles; alternate device families while preserving a common physical law; use negative space as pacing; move between emotional and rational modes without abandoning the visual world; design the footer as a final scene; scale motion intensity to the visitor's task instead of imposing the landing-page grammar on reading pages; let navigation and overlays inherit the material law rather than look like generic UI laid over the experience.
- Production/assets implied: Brand-motif generator or shader/mask system, high-resolution architectural renders, graded location imagery, motion-ready cutouts, semantic content layers, carousel states, and a scroll timeline capable of stable masking and pinning.
- Codex desktop/OpenAI workflow translation: Codex should first extract a brand-specific material law and scene grammar, then use it to propose transitions and asset requirements. It should reject effects that do not obey that law. Implementation planning can still include CSS masks, canvas/WebGL, image sequences, and GSAP, but the creative direction precedes the library choice.
- Evidence locations/timestamps: Homepage captured at scroll positions 0, ~3.3k, ~7.9k, ~13.5k, ~19.1k, ~24.7k, and ~27.4k of ~28.1k px. - User-specific preference: “Every part is perfect” is supported as coordination, not one spectacular effect: image selection, motif recurrence, typography contrast, pacing, chapter topology, utility, and page-specific restraint all reinforce the same identity.
- Transferable principles: Build a site around a productive tension in the subject (precision/personality, public/private); let the opening visual proposition return transformed at the close; use a limited accent color as an active transition material; choreograph photographs in spatial relationships rather than merely animating individual cards; make content categories behave differently while keeping shared motifs; design data-heavy and legal surfaces as members of the same world instead of forcing landing-page spectacle onto them.

- Codex desktop/OpenAI workflow translation: Codex should identify a brand tension and propose a beginning-to-end visual transformation before choosing effects. It should map each image asset to a narrative role and plan match cuts or spatial relationships between assets. OpenAI image/video generation can fill missing scenes only after the real asset taxonomy and crop requirements are defined.

- Composition and hierarchy: Most frames have one dominant visual fact. Full-bleed portraits give way to nearly empty white sheets; centered product cutouts cross those boundaries; large central crops are balanced by smaller edge crops; story text is isolated in generous columns; the collection becomes a horizontal visual catalogue rather than a card grid. Persistent utilities hug the edges and leave the central stage open.
- Spacing, scale, density, and visual rhythm: The design is spacious because density is sequenced rather than eliminated. It alternates image saturation, white editorial silence, object focus, reading space, audio pause, and catalogue density. Each chapter is long, but the repeated internal cadence creates confidence and orientation rather than monotony.

- Motion grammar and input mapping: Scroll changes the topology of the composition. A full-bleed portrait is overlaid by a rising white editorial plane; a ring cutout remains visually continuous while surrounding images reframe and resize; side crops enter to counterweight a large center image; type appears as part of a sheet or stage rather than as independent reveal effects. The chapter rail remains fixed as an orientation instrument. Chapter links perform large but controlled spatial jumps into matching portrait thresholds.
- Scene connections and transitions: The featured jewelry object is used as a match-cut anchor across otherwise different crops and layouts. White planes physically cover or uncover imagery, causing one scene to become the next. Repeated bracket labels and the fixed bottom chapter rail preserve context while the center composition changes dramatically. Maker and contact states arrive as large editorial panels embedded over the current image world, not generic floating dialogs.
- Engineered peak and resolution: The cumulative peak is structural: after five individually complete tales, the final black scene states “From five, everything begins,” turning repetition into a completed system. - What creates polish: Every motion decision changes hierarchy, framing, or narrative focus; the product acts as a visual protagonist; a stable chapter cadence supports extensive variation; typography and utility elements remain extremely restrained; fixed navigation provides orientation; imagery shares one art direction; overlays inherit the editorial geometry; optional sound is framed as a chapter-specific layer rather than autoplay spectacle.

- Transferable principles: Design one repeatable narrative score, then art-direct each chapter as a variation; allow a product or symbol to persist across scene boundaries; use scroll to change composition, not to decorate elements; pair a stable orientation layer with an evolving stage; sequence dense and sparse scenes; treat overlays as spatial continuations of the page; make long-form repetition feel ceremonial through controlled variation.
- Production/assets implied: Five coherent portrait/editorial shoots, isolated high-resolution jewelry cutouts, responsive crop plans, a custom display type/wordmark system, optional chapter audio, image-sequence or mask-ready assets, and a robust scroll timeline with pinned rails and scene-specific transforms.
- Codex desktop/OpenAI workflow translation: Codex should begin with a narrative score for each repeated unit, then demand a distinct emotional and compositional variation for every instance. It should specify the persistent visual anchor, dominant focus per frame, sparse/dense pacing, crop relationships, overlay geometry, and optional sound role before naming implementation tools. Generated assets should be commissioned as a coordinated shot list, not prompted one image at a time.
