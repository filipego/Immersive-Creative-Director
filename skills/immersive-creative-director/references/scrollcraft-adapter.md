# Scrollcraft compatibility adapter

Scrollcraft is the primary specialist for scroll intelligence. Immersive Creative Director remains the sole creative/taste authority. Full invocation means execute Scrollcraft's relevant workflow and produce its required planning/build evidence; it does not mean importing Scrollcraft's house assumptions into every subject.

## Precedence

Apply instructions in this order:

1. Current project facts, explicit user decisions, protected visual locks, and authorization.
2. Immersive corpus authority, subject truth, experience contract, and approved direction.
3. This adapter's classification of Scrollcraft rules.
4. Scrollcraft's applicable planning, implementation, and verification mechanics.

When a lower rule conflicts with a higher one, record the conflict and resolution in `IMMERSIVE-DIRECTION-RUN.json`. Do not silently follow both, average them, or treat the specialist's default as evidence that the concept should change.

## Always retain from Scrollcraft

- Real human interview inputs, integrated into the mandatory Grilling frontier rather than duplicated.
- Visitor journey, belief/action outcome, feeling curve, one earned peak, contrast/silence, and tell-someone sentence.
- Explicit grammar selection with rejected alternatives.
- One subject-specific signature move.
- Scroll score: one semantic job per beat, authored span, input/hold/release, reversal, orientation, normal-flow relief, responsive route, reduced-motion meaning, and feasibility.
- Chronological desktop/mobile/reduced-motion capture, dead-scroll detection, feel check, and correction loop during production.
- Real semantic content and direct inspection of generated assets and rendered states.

## Treat as conditional implementation defaults

These rules apply only when they strengthen the approved subject-specific direction. They are never universal concept requirements:

- Four or more device families.
- Never repeating a device family in adjacent scenes.
- At most two scrub passages.
- Eight to fourteen viewport heights.
- Photographic art direction as the default world.
- A fixed number of acts, one particular grammar family, or a registry-derived novelty threshold.
- Specific navigation, hero, counter, cue, type, or close prohibitions.
- Kie.ai, Scrollcraft's engine/template, or any named asset provider.

For each conditional default, record `adopted`, `adapted`, or `rejected`, the project evidence, and the resulting behavior. Rejecting a default does not skip Scrollcraft; it demonstrates reconciliation.

Examples: a ceremonial story may intentionally repeat one mechanism while its emotional/compositional state changes; an illustrated brand may remain illustrated; a Pear-like causal journey may exceed fourteen viewports; a quiet editorial experience may need two device families rather than four. The test is subject ownership, visitor understanding, causal continuity, agency, and rendered quality—not numerical compliance with another skill's prior-build fingerprint.

## Provider and approval containment

Scrollcraft's asset-generation commands are never implicit authority. The Immersive source, still-generation, state-board, media/provider, cost, regeneration, build, and release approvals remain in force. Use Codex/OpenAI image generation where appropriate; use another provider only after the applicable capability and approval gate. Never run a provider probe, spend credits, upload sources, or generate media merely because Scrollcraft lists a command.

## Completion evidence

When scroll is material, `IMMERSIVE-DIRECTION-RUN.json` must set `adapterApplied: true` and contain the journey beats, feeling curve, peak, selected grammar, signature move, scroll score, input contract, responsive plan, reduced-motion parity, and verdict. It must also record every conditional Scrollcraft rule whose adoption would materially affect the concept. Missing reconciliation blocks concept advancement.
