# Production

Production turns an approved direction into the smallest truthful system that can deliver it. Begin from the exact approved version of the passed [experience contract](experience-contract.md): route inventory, copy provenance, atomic responsibility map, and complete state storyboard are immutable acceptance inputs unless the user explicitly approves a `SCOPE-CHANGE-*` amendment. Production cannot invent missing public copy, create decorative routes, restore responsibilities omitted by the concept, or reduce a broader scoped experience to one approved fragment. Select the substrate from the first dependency that must be reliable; do not select a stack, generator, or effect because it is familiar or impressive. This reference supplies build-readiness inputs. It does not certify a rendered experience; use `QUALITY-rendered-*` for that verdict.

## 1. Choose the production substrate

Name one primary record and why its nearest alternative loses value or control.

### `PROD-interface-led`

Choose when navigation, evidence, editorial reading, comparison, booking, forms, or live product utility frames the value. Establish page modes, content hierarchy, semantic and responsive structure, utility behavior, and the visual lock before adding experiential motion. Use media as proof or support. Reject this route when a scene chronology or spatial object must be canonical first; reject it also when it becomes a pretext for generic card and reveal animation.

### `PROD-media-led`

Choose when an authored sequence, performance, documentary passage, or transformation carries the main story. Establish the concept spine, ordered beats, approved canonical states, copy-safe regions, crops, and segment transitions before deriving interface orientation and conversion from the media world. Reject it when film is only a decorative backdrop, authentic proof or task utility must lead, or continuity cannot be controlled at a justified cost.

### `PROD-object-world-led`

Choose when a product, character, space, field, or simulation must remain spatially true across states. Establish geometry/topology, canonical views, materials, light, camera, state inventory, and a performance prototype before dependent media or interface claims. Reject decorative world-building with no visitor job, unreliable source truth, absent topology, or an untested device path.

### `PROD-deliberate-hybrid`

Choose only when no single substrate can carry the approved concept. Draw a dependency graph that names the canonical owner for identity, spatial truth, chronology, interface, proof, and conversion; every layer needs a non-overlapping job. A stable interface plus a field, authentic proof plus connective plates, or editorial routes plus one media chapter can qualify. Reject “hybrid” when parallel systems invent different worlds, duplicate an interaction, or spend performance on unsupported variety.

## 2. Select a medium by its irreplaceable job

Compare only plausible media. State the job, minimum control, primary risk, and fallback.

- Authentic assets carry product, people, place, community, and documentary truth. Audit rights, resolution, variants, crops, and grade; retain them unless a real gap requires an extension.
- Generated stills supply controlled missing states or original art direction. Require an asset bible, state IDs, crop variants, and approval; never substitute synthetic imagery for needed evidence.
- Layered still scenes earn depth, occlusion, masks, and economical movement only with separate planes, copy-safe zones, and a depth/camera contract. Fall back to the flat editorial state if seams or cardboard parallax weaken it.
- Image sequences earn frame-precise transformation or scrub control. Require approved keyframes, preload plan, mobile treatment, and static key-state order; reduce frames or use discrete states when payload or continuity fails.
- Short clips earn time-specific action that stills cannot communicate. Lock start/end states, action, camera, duration, loop/exit, and regeneration boundary. Use the endpoints plus a layered transition if connection fails.
- Long video earns a real performance/documentary or a genuinely controllable continuous journey. Require storyboard/animatic, responsive edits, loading strategy, segmentation, and explicit approval. Prefer approved short chapters or sequences when continuity is less controllable.
- 3D earns inspection, configuration, reusable spatial states, or controlled camera truth. Require geometry, material/light, interaction/camera contract, performance prototype, and canonical-render fallback.
- A procedural/WebGL field earns multiplicity, data, terrain, particles, or spatial type only with source geometry, density states, camera, legibility zones, ceiling, and 2D/stateful fallback.
- DOM/CSS/SVG earns semantic interface hierarchy, responsive structure, masks, paths, and accessible interaction. Define its motion contract and reduced-motion static composition.
- Sound is optional atmosphere or causal feedback. Provide user control, cue map, captions/transcript where meaningful, and complete silent parity.

Do not enumerate formats after the dependency is clear. The simpler candidate wins unless the selected medium performs a named job it cannot.

## 3. Establish trustworthy source material

Run an asset audit before generation or build. Inventory truth-bearing brand/product/people/place assets; rights; format, resolution, aspect ratio, alpha/layers; crop and copy-safe regions; existing tokens, grade, motion, and visual locks; canonical subjects/locations and inconsistent variants; required story states; responsive variants; heavy-media risks; and fallback candidates. Identify what is reusable, transformable, extendable, regenerable, or fixed. Do not commission an item merely because it appears in a shot list.

For continuity-critical production, create a proportional asset bible. It names subject identity and allowed variation; product geometry, logos, materials, and deformation limits; environment geography, adjacency, landmarks, time, weather, and light; camera language; palette/texture/grade; canonical-source authority order; responsive focal anchors and copy-safe zones; named states, start/end frames, and invalid states; plus static and reduced-motion narrative parity. A single interface mask does not need a film bible; a continuity-driven sequence does.

Approve a numbered contact sheet or canonical model/views before any interpolation, dependent transition, or long-motion generation. Prompt resemblance is not continuity evidence.

## 4. Specify scenes, space, and camera

Write a shot manifest only for meaningful segments. Each `SHOT-*` record must cite its governing `STATE-ID`, `RESP-ID`, `ROUTE-ID`, and every applicable `COPY-ID`, then contain: surface mode and visitor/business job; start and end state; dominant and supporting mechanism; source and continuity reference; framing/depth/topology; copy-safe/interface state; input contract (scroll span, hold, release, reversibility); desktop/mobile/fallback treatment; method; dependency and approval status; load implication; and entry/exit transition. Merge or delete a scene that changes no state or performs no distinct job.

When the visitor must believe a coherent space, add `PROD-topology`: adjacency, entry/exit direction, scale, landmarks, windows/views, object placement, and return state. A mood board is not topology.

When virtual travel is promised, add `PROD-camera-path`: named route, start/end framing, direction, lens and height, thresholds, speed logic, occlusion, copy moments, and resolution/return. “Cinematic fly-through” is not a camera path. Generate dependent movement only after these records and the contact sheet agree.

## 5. Art-direct responsive and fallback experiences

For every relevant page mode, specify desktop and mobile framing, focal anchor, crop, density, scene count, controls/input, asset variant, loading/performance ceiling, and conversion/utility continuity. Mobile is a separate production, not a scaled desktop crop. Record intentional differences and their preserved narrative job.

Define a fallback for unavailable heavy media and a reduced-motion/static route that retains opening-to-resolution order, required evidence, orientation, and conversion. If the story only works with the preferred GPU, warm cache, or one desktop crop, it is not production-ready. Preserve usable semantics and controls underneath the experiential layer.

## 6. Choose fidelity honestly

Use `PROD-lean` for existing assets, generated stills, layered depth, compact sequences/clips, and one concentrated peak over a strong editorial/utility base. Use `PROD-studio` for coordinated still sets, responsive variants, several approved transitions, richer custom behavior, limited world work with a named job, and deeper rendered iteration. Use `PROD-flagship` for continuity-controlled film, extensive frame systems, custom worlds/fields/spatial audio, separately produced responsive versions, and corresponding performance and approval work. Tier describes coordination and fidelity, never a presumed budget or creative merit.

## 7. Keep approval payloads distinct

Direction approval names the protected kernel, coherence source, visitor/business job, story score, peak/resolution, intended page modes, ambition, and risks. It permits reversible planning, not continuity-dependent production.

Continuity-source approval identifies canonical stills/models/topology/characters/objects/environments/crops, fixed versus variable properties, and dependent scenes. It precedes dependent motion.

Media approval is required for costly video, complex 3D, or optional external media. It states why stills/layers/sequences lose; tool; expected cost or credit use; source assets; scope and regeneration boundary; continuity risk; and fallback. It does not authorize a different service, unlimited generations, or release.

Build authorization names the target surface, approved direction, visual lock, approved/generable assets, implementation scope, local verification scope, and whether external release is separately included. A locked visual design requires explicit approval for any visible layout, spacing, type, color, sizing, imagery, or presentation divergence. Publication, deployment, purchase, submission, and contact always require their own exact action authority.

## `PROD-build-readiness` verdict

Mark build-ready only when the exact approved JSON passes the bundled validator with `--phase build` and the approved direction, all four experience-contract artifacts, dependency order, substrate, asset audit, required bible/contact sheet/topology/camera records, manifest, responsive and fallback treatments, approval state, risks, and realistic implementation route agree. Every `ROUTE-ID`, `RESP-ID`, `COPY-ID`, and `STATE-ID` must have an implementation owner and verification target. Mark the material unknown that blocks any of those claims; continue unaffected reversible planning. Production readiness never substitutes for rendered chronological proof.
