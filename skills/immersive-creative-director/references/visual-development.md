# Universal visual development

Every approved Direction or Transformation must become visible before full production, including editorial DOM/SVG, layered-still, procedural, authentic-media, 3D, and visually locked-layout work. Still-first previsualization remains the deeper continuity protocol for connected generated motion; this package proves the complete design world even when that protocol is not applicable.

After concept approval and explicit visual-development authority, create `IMMERSIVE-VISUAL-DEVELOPMENT.json` beside the direction run and experience contract. Produce a coordinated board, not unrelated mood options.

## System proof

Define and show the approved territory's:

- grid and alignment behavior across page modes;
- spacing rhythm, density cadence, and silence;
- typography roles, scale relationships, measure, and motion role;
- palette/material law and contrast behavior;
- imagery treatment, grade, crop, focal anchors, and copy-safe relationships;
- motion law and relationship to the story score;
- sound policy, including complete silent parity.

Every choice must cite subject/corpus evidence and exclude recognizable reference identity.

## Five required style-frame roles

Create inspectable desktop and mobile evidence for:

1. `opening` — establishes promise, orientation, and world.
2. `quiet-editorial` — proves reading, proof, or recovery without becoming generic remainder.
3. `peak` — proves the earned largest change and its hierarchy.
4. `decision-utility` — proves navigation, form, comparison, booking, contact, or other real visitor task belongs to the world.
5. `resolution` — proves the ending answers the opening and holds.

A single frame may serve two roles only when the canonical experience genuinely combines them; record both roles and why. Each frame maps to real contract states/responsibilities/copy, has a composition job, and receives a `strong`, `needs work`, or `reject` verdict.

## Representative motion study

Prototype the highest-risk or most identity-bearing transition before full build. It can be coded, layered, procedural, an image sequence, approved authentic media, 3D, or a low-cost animatic. It must demonstrate the signature move or the hardest composition/input handoff—not a convenient text reveal.

Inspect desktop, mobile, reduced-motion parity, and reversal/exit when applicable. Record start/end state IDs, why this study is representative, direct evidence, defects, corrections, and verdict. If the study fails, simplify or change the mechanism before producing the rest of the site.

## Approval and validation

Present the complete system board and representative motion study as one versioned approval target. Approval of a hero or isolated peak does not approve the package.

Run:

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_visual_development.py /absolute/path/IMMERSIVE-VISUAL-DEVELOPMENT.json
```

Then link the strong manifest from `IMMERSIVE-DIRECTION-RUN.json` and validate the run at `--phase visual-development`. Full build authorization remains separate.
