# Quality gates

Quality gates are blocking diagnostic lenses, not a scorecard. For every applicable gate retain an internal record: stable ID, verdict (`strong`, `needs work`, or `reject`), direct evidence, affected concept/scene/route/state, smallest correction or rejection reason, dependency/approval required, and regression to rerun. A serious defect blocks presentation, build-readiness, or completion even if other gates are strong.

## 1. Reject weak directions before presenting them

Name the protected kernel: the user's starting idea, subject truth, approved layout/visual lock, desired feeling, and explicit constraints. Generate three genuinely different coherence sources, not three treatments. Test each, reject the weakest, develop two survivors as chronological visitor journeys, then recommend one. Present no knowingly weak decoy or numerical scorecard.

Use these direction gates:

- `QUALITY-corpus-authority`: every mandatory core file was read completely in the current invocation; every user-named reference was matched to its detailed website/video evidence; each judgment has the required evidence lineage and transfer boundary; and no generic pretrained taste, majority-web convention, default AI pattern, trend memory, or unstated outside reference entered aesthetic reasoning at any stage. Reject when the model cannot explain a named corpus source, treats evidence as optional inspiration, invents missing chronology, uses its own aesthetic instinct as fallback or tie-breaker, or produces a direction that would remain unchanged after its claimed evidence is removed. This is a blocking gate with no discretionary bypass.
- `QUALITY-protocol-compliance`: every workflow required by the selected mode must have direct evidence that its complete instructions, supporting resources, interaction rounds, waits, artifacts, and completion criterion ran. For Direction and Transformation, reject concept output unless the Grilling + Domain Modeling decision frontier is empty and the user confirmed the shared understanding. Reading a skill, paraphrasing it, or substituting ordinary questions is a protocol failure.
- `QUALITY-executable-run`: the current `IMMERSIVE-DIRECTION-RUN.json` persists corpus grounding, intake, evidence lineage, territories, Scrollcraft reconciliation, linked artifacts, dependencies, quality evidence, and blockers; its validator passes at the claimed phase. Reject memory-only ledgers, stale versions, absent user-confirmation evidence, or a supporting artifact not linked into the run.
- `QUALITY-decision-memory`: every approved site-wide system, page, section, component, transition, and responsive state has a current human-readable dossier record with embedded stable visual evidence, exact reproducible specifications, direct approval evidence, adjacent handoffs, linked artifact versions, and preserved supersession history. Reject advancement when conversation memory, code, a screenshot alone, or scattered JSON records are the only record of an approval.
- `QUALITY-source-lock`: every visitor-visible string has a `COPY-ID`, exact text, one legal provenance value, exact authority, transformation status, uses, and verdict. Reject internal strategy/discovery language presented as public copy, model-authored text labeled approved, an unmarked placeholder in a visual, or any “temporary” string without provenance.
- `QUALITY-route-integrity`: `CONTRACT-route-inventory` accounts for every route, chapter, navigation item, external destination, and required handoff. Reject invented routes, decorative navigation, unresolved labels/destinations, inaccessible required destinations, or a focus request treated as a silent scope amendment.
- `QUALITY-content-coverage`: `CONTRACT-responsibility-map` atomizes every canonical obligation and individually required record, represents collections through source/selection/ordering/reachability/fallback contracts, maps each obligation to routes and chronological states, and reconciles to zero unmapped or invented IDs across desktop, mobile, reduced motion, and fallback. Reject umbrella rows that conceal named required items or any promise that implementation will restore omitted content.
- `QUALITY-state-storyboard`: every surviving territory has a real-evidence storyboard covering the complete scoped experience from opening through resolution and required handoffs. Each immersive passage has at least three consecutive causal states; every required `RESP-ID` appears; and calm/utility passages retain intentional composition and continuity. Reject a focused fragment presented as the broader direction, attractive keyframes, abstract section names, or one authored passage surrounded by a generic remainder.
- `QUALITY-contract-validation`: the canonical JSON for the current contract version passes the bundled validator at the claimed phase. Reject stale validation, a different contract version, manual substitution, ignored validator errors, or an unavailable runtime. A structure pass is not an approval pass; an approval pass is not a build pass.
- `QUALITY-advancement-language`: before the complete canonical-scope contract reaches `approval-candidate` with all four artifacts strong, the work is an `exploratory fragment`, `incomplete territory`, or `blocked direction`. Reject `recommended direction`, `approved concept`, `winning territory`, build handoff, or next-phase language applied to partial work. Reject approval requests that hide contract version, approval target, route/coverage/state counts, placeholders, blockers, or exclusions.
- `QUALITY-protected-kernel`: additions must strengthen the named kernel. Reject a reference-shaped spectacle or a premise that replaces the user's idea.
- `QUALITY-subject-specificity`: subject objects, verbs, tensions, evidence, process, culture, audience, or business job must generate the direction. Reject a concept that survives a brand-name swap.
- `QUALITY-transplantation`: identify what breaks when moved unchanged to an unrelated subject. Reject when only palette, logo, copy, or imagery changes.
- `QUALITY-coherence-source`: one premise must govern varied composition, motion, imagery, transitions, orientation, and resolution. Reject an effects list, adjective, or repetitive visual filter.
- `QUALITY-story-causality`: every meaningful beat prepares, causes, transforms, proves, or resolves. Reject freely reorderable scenes, concealed unrelated cuts, or duration standing in for progression.
- `QUALITY-motion-necessity`: every major mechanism needs an owned job a still cannot do and its source condition. Reject mechanisms retained solely for “visual interest.”
- `QUALITY-composition`: hierarchy, spacing, scale, focus, density, legibility, and image relationships must remain authored through motion. Reject fragmented attention, crowded frames, and independently animated layout parts.
- `QUALITY-intensity-subtraction`: after composing the journey, name the protected focal event in every consequential state and remove, subordinate, or quiet every competing accent, layer, contrast event, scale jump, and simultaneous movement that does not strengthen it. Preserve personality and intentional peaks; reject continuous loudness, accumulated decoration, equal emphasis, or quietness that flattens hierarchy into generic minimalism.
- `QUALITY-agency-orientation`: progress, reversal/exit, invariant frame, holds/releases, feedback, and fallback comprehension must be explicit. Reject trapped wheel/touch, unjustified pinning, or lost orientation.
- `QUALITY-whole-site`: all relevant discovery, proof, reading, decision, utility, navigation, support, legal, and ending modes need an intentional relationship to the world. Reject one authored passage surrounded by a generic remainder.
- `QUALITY-peak-resolution`: the peak must be prepared by contrast and followed by conversion, release, or an ending that resolves the opening promise. Reject continuous escalation, an overlay CTA, or an abrupt stop.

At major concept, visual-development, and rendered-experience gates, form the verdict through two independent readings before synthesis: a **creative-direction lens** for concept ownership, emotional effect, composition, continuity, originality, and controlled intensity; and a **visitor-experience lens** for orientation, comprehension, agency, pacing, interruption, utility, and meaning. Reconcile disagreements, then lead with the highest-leverage correction. Preserve what is strong; do not polish details while the governing premise, composition, or journey should be rejected.

## 2. Decide conceptual sufficiency and production readiness

`QUALITY-conceptual-sufficiency` is strong only when corpus authority, protocol compliance, the executable direction run, source lock, route integrity, atomic content coverage, complete state storyboard, current validation, advancement language, the protected kernel, specificity, coherence, causality, and motion necessity are strong; no blocking composition, agency, whole-site, peak/resolution, production, responsive, authorization, dependency, or orchestration weakness remains; and the asset/build path is feasible at the chosen tier. Remaining choices must be genuine preferences or bounded implementation refinements. This authorizes only the next approved phase, never a claim of rendered polish.

Use these production gates before build:

- `QUALITY-substrate-fit`: selected interface/media/world/hybrid order follows dependency and visitor value; the selected medium has a job a simpler route cannot do. Reject tutorial, novelty, or capability-led stacks.
- `QUALITY-visual-development`: the approved territory has a passing universal visual-development manifest proving the system plus opening, quiet/editorial, peak, decision/utility, and resolution compositions on desktop and mobile; its highest-risk representative motion study passes desktop, mobile, reduced-motion, and reversal/exit inspection. Reject a hero-only board, unrelated mood options, typography/color tokens without compositions, or full production before the representative transition works.
- `QUALITY-performance-budget`: a passing project-specific budget names target devices/inputs, positive media/runtime ceilings, loading and responsive asset strategy, low-power/no-WebGL/reduced-motion/heavy-media failure routes, measurement plan, and approval. Reject “optimize later,” desktop-only assumptions, warm-cache proof, or undefined heavy-media limits.
- `QUALITY-dependency-compatibility`: every selected specialist is present, current, fully loaded, reconciled through its adapter/boundary, and provides its required outputs. Reject name-only invocation, changed/unverified dependency behavior, or a specialist default that overrides the protected subject/corpus.
- `QUALITY-asset-continuity`: authentic sources, canonical states, crops, topology/camera where applicable, source approval, and regeneration boundaries support the promise. Reject assumed long-form generated continuity, travel without topology, or dependent motion from unapproved states.
- `QUALITY-previsualization`: whenever generated motion depends on connected visual states, the exact [previsualization.md](previsualization.md) manifest and approved experience contract must cross-validate; every dependent contract/shot endpoint must map one-to-one to a full-size inspectable still with a real, structurally decodable local media file; authentic sources or an explicitly original/canonical source mode and anchor must be locked; the complete numbered contact sheet must be a separate local image and carry direct approval; every required adjacent/branch edge must be unique, connected, responsive, and covered; and the applicable state-board, motion, and build validator phases must pass. External/video edges additionally require a named approved provider, typed cost/credit ceiling, integer regeneration limit, controlled fallback, and direct first/intermediate/last/handoff review. Reject a text storyboard, one hero image presented as a sequence, fabricated asset IDs or URLs, a one-state/zero-edge manifest, duplicated visual content under different paths, skipped or orphaned endpoints, a provider's self-reported consistency, an unnamed tool delegation, a beautiful clip with endpoint drift, or a desktop asset whose mobile crop loses meaning.
- `QUALITY-responsive-parity`: relevant viewports and inputs have separate framing, density, controls, asset/load treatment, and narrative-parity static/reduced-motion sequence. Reject scaled desktop, hidden subject, or removed meaning/conversion.
- `QUALITY-buildability`: dependencies, minimum source set, risks, performance/tool limits, optional upgrades, time assumptions, and honest unknowns are bounded. Reject plans that rely on perfect AI output, undefined external services, unlimited regeneration, or invisible user labor.
- `QUALITY-authorization`: record current authority, reversible work that can continue, next consequential boundary, exact approval payload, and unaffected work. Reject treating concept/local-build approval as authority to redesign a lock, spend, choose paid media, publish, deploy, submit, purchase, or contact.
- `QUALITY-skill-orchestration`: relevant specialists must have a recorded predicate and a single creative/dispatch owner; invoke them fully and avoid duplicate critics or premise-setting implementation agents. Reject a generic skills list or an unowned build route.

`QUALITY-build-readiness` is strong only when conceptual sufficiency and all applicable production gates agree with the approved substrate, universal visual-development package, representative motion study, project-specific performance budget, current dependency compatibility, assets and continuity artifacts, page/shot specifications, applicable previsualization manifest and validator evidence, responsive/fallback plan, approvals, and implementation route. Material unknowns are blockers; bounded implementation detail is not.

## 3. Verify an actual experience in chronological order

Rendered gates apply only to an accessible target. Source code, automated tests, asset sheets, or keyframe screenshots may inform a diagnosis but cannot pass them. Inspect the actual surface from opening through resolution, including route handoffs and failure recovery.

- `QUALITY-rendered-chronology`: observe load/opening response; each transition; copy timing; peak setup, hold, and release; interactive states; conversion; route handoffs; and final resolution. Reject completion on attractive keyframes alone, dead frames, early exits, jumps, or unresolved state.
- `QUALITY-rendered-composition`: inspect focal hierarchy, crop, spacing, type, contrast, overlap, density, legibility, and visual lock at live states and relevant viewports. Source values are not proof of the rendered relationship.
- `QUALITY-rendered-input`: exercise available wheel/trackpad/touch/keyboard/pointer paths, holds/releases, reversal, progress, focus, restoration, and motion preference. Reject trapped scroll, lost focus, ambiguous input obstruction, or a fallback that removes required information.
- `QUALITY-rendered-performance`: test cold/load behavior, media readiness, layout stability, frame behavior, responsive asset selection, realistic device constraints, and unavailable-heavy-media recovery. Reject a result that only works warm, on a flagship device, or after a hidden loading failure.
- `QUALITY-rendered-utility`: verify navigation, links, decision/conversion actions, pricing/booking/forms, support/legal, and relevant loading, empty, and error states. Reject a surface whose immersive moment works while the visitor task is broken, generic, or unverified.

When evidence is unavailable, label the gate not applicable or limited; do not convert an uninspected claim into a pass. Distinguish a verified failure from an evidence limitation, and give the smallest next evidence action rather than a speculative diagnosis.

## 4. Issue the completion verdict

`QUALITY-exact-surface-completion` may be strong only when all applicable rendered gates pass on the exact claimed target; the observed chronology matches the approved story and visual lock; relevant routes, states, viewports, inputs, fallbacks, and utility behavior have direct current evidence; and any required external release was verified on that released/submitted/message surface. Local verification cannot establish deployed fidelity. Screenshots support composition only; they do not prove chronology, input, performance, forms/errors, or route utility.

Completion must state the surface and scope proved, evidence limits, and any remaining issue that is explicitly accepted or outside authority. Do not call an experience finished merely because concept, assets, code, builds, or isolated screenshots look strong.

## 5. Audit authorization without stopping safe work

Treat authority as scoped: direction, continuity source, costly media, visual divergence, local build, and external mutation are separate. Continue reversible in-scope analysis, specification, and local evidence collection. Pause only at the next choice that materially changes the kernel, commits dependent production to an unapproved source, changes a locked presentation, spends on optional media, or creates external state.

For any external action, the approval record must identify the exact destination/account, action/content, spend cap where relevant, recipient/data where relevant, and final-surface verification. Execute only that named action; do not expand to adjacent publishing, purchase, submission, or contact. Once authority is specific, do not manufacture repeated confirmation.

After a gate or authorization rule changes, rerun its direct regression plus an adjacent one. A gate lacking direct evidence is a validation defect, not a pass.
