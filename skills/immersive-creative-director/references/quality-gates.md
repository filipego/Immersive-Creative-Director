# Quality gates

Quality gates are blocking diagnostic lenses, not a scorecard. For every applicable gate retain an internal record: stable ID, verdict (`strong`, `needs work`, or `reject`), direct evidence, affected concept/scene/route/state, smallest correction or rejection reason, dependency/approval required, and regression to rerun. A serious defect blocks presentation, build-readiness, or completion even if other gates are strong.

## 1. Reject weak directions before presenting them

Name the protected kernel: the user's starting idea, subject truth, approved layout/visual lock, desired feeling, and explicit constraints. Generate three genuinely different coherence sources, not three treatments. Test each, reject the weakest, develop two survivors as chronological visitor journeys, then recommend one. Present no knowingly weak decoy or numerical scorecard.

Use these direction gates:

- `QUALITY-protected-kernel`: additions must strengthen the named kernel. Reject a reference-shaped spectacle or a premise that replaces the user's idea.
- `QUALITY-subject-specificity`: subject objects, verbs, tensions, evidence, process, culture, audience, or business job must generate the direction. Reject a concept that survives a brand-name swap.
- `QUALITY-transplantation`: identify what breaks when moved unchanged to an unrelated subject. Reject when only palette, logo, copy, or imagery changes.
- `QUALITY-coherence-source`: one premise must govern varied composition, motion, imagery, transitions, orientation, and resolution. Reject an effects list, adjective, or repetitive visual filter.
- `QUALITY-story-causality`: every meaningful beat prepares, causes, transforms, proves, or resolves. Reject freely reorderable scenes, concealed unrelated cuts, or duration standing in for progression.
- `QUALITY-motion-necessity`: every major mechanism needs an owned job a still cannot do and its source condition. Reject mechanisms retained solely for “visual interest.”
- `QUALITY-composition`: hierarchy, spacing, scale, focus, density, legibility, and image relationships must remain authored through motion. Reject fragmented attention, crowded frames, and independently animated layout parts.
- `QUALITY-agency-orientation`: progress, reversal/exit, invariant frame, holds/releases, feedback, and fallback comprehension must be explicit. Reject trapped wheel/touch, unjustified pinning, or lost orientation.
- `QUALITY-whole-site`: all relevant discovery, proof, reading, decision, utility, navigation, support, legal, and ending modes need an intentional relationship to the world. Reject a directed hero with generic remainder.
- `QUALITY-peak-resolution`: the peak must be prepared by contrast and followed by conversion, release, or an ending that resolves the opening promise. Reject continuous escalation, an overlay CTA, or an abrupt stop.

Lead any rejection with the most damaging issue, evidence, and smallest correction. Preserve what is strong; do not polish a premise that should be rejected.

## 2. Decide conceptual sufficiency and production readiness

`QUALITY-conceptual-sufficiency` is strong only when the protected kernel, specificity, coherence, causality, and motion necessity are strong; no blocking composition, agency, whole-site, peak/resolution, production, responsive, authorization, or orchestration weakness remains; and the asset/build path is feasible at the chosen tier. Remaining choices must be genuine preferences or bounded implementation refinements. This authorizes only the next approved phase, never a claim of rendered polish.

Use these production gates before build:

- `QUALITY-substrate-fit`: selected interface/media/world/hybrid order follows dependency and visitor value; the selected medium has a job a simpler route cannot do. Reject tutorial, novelty, or capability-led stacks.
- `QUALITY-asset-continuity`: authentic sources, canonical states, crops, topology/camera where applicable, source approval, and regeneration boundaries support the promise. Reject assumed long-form generated continuity, travel without topology, or dependent motion from unapproved states.
- `QUALITY-responsive-parity`: relevant viewports and inputs have separate framing, density, controls, asset/load treatment, and narrative-parity static/reduced-motion sequence. Reject scaled desktop, hidden subject, or removed meaning/conversion.
- `QUALITY-buildability`: dependencies, minimum source set, risks, performance/tool limits, optional upgrades, time assumptions, and honest unknowns are bounded. Reject plans that rely on perfect AI output, undefined external services, unlimited regeneration, or invisible user labor.
- `QUALITY-authorization`: record current authority, reversible work that can continue, next consequential boundary, exact approval payload, and unaffected work. Reject treating concept/local-build approval as authority to redesign a lock, spend, choose paid media, publish, deploy, submit, purchase, or contact.
- `QUALITY-skill-orchestration`: relevant specialists must have a recorded predicate and a single creative/dispatch owner; invoke them fully and avoid duplicate critics or premise-setting implementation agents. Reject a generic skills list or an unowned build route.

`QUALITY-build-readiness` is strong only when conceptual sufficiency and all applicable production gates agree with the approved substrate, assets and continuity artifacts, page/shot specifications, responsive/fallback plan, approvals, and implementation route. Material unknowns are blockers; bounded implementation detail is not.

## 3. Verify an actual experience in chronological order

Rendered gates apply only to an accessible target. Source code, automated tests, asset sheets, or keyframe screenshots may inform a diagnosis but cannot pass them. Inspect the actual surface from opening through resolution, including route handoffs and failure recovery.

- `QUALITY-rendered-chronology`: observe load/opening response; each transition; copy timing; peak setup, hold, and release; interactive states; conversion; route handoffs; and final resolution. Reject completion on attractive keyframes alone, dead frames, early exits, jumps, or unresolved state.
- `QUALITY-rendered-composition`: inspect focal hierarchy, crop, spacing, type, contrast, overlap, density, legibility, and visual lock at live states and relevant viewports. Source values are not proof of the rendered relationship.
- `QUALITY-rendered-input`: exercise available wheel/trackpad/touch/keyboard/pointer paths, holds/releases, reversal, progress, focus, restoration, and motion preference. Reject trapped scroll, lost focus, ambiguous input obstruction, or a fallback that removes required information.
- `QUALITY-rendered-performance`: test cold/load behavior, media readiness, layout stability, frame behavior, responsive asset selection, realistic device constraints, and unavailable-heavy-media recovery. Reject a result that only works warm, on a flagship device, or after a hidden loading failure.
- `QUALITY-rendered-utility`: verify navigation, links, decision/conversion actions, pricing/booking/forms, support/legal, and relevant loading, empty, and error states. Reject a surface whose immersive moment works while the visitor task is broken, generic, or unverified.

When evidence is unavailable, label the gate not applicable or limited; do not convert an uninspected claim into a pass. Distinguish a verified failure from an evidence limitation, and give the smallest next evidence action rather than a speculative diagnosis.

## 4. Issue the completion verdict

`QUALITY-exact-surface-completion` may be strong only when all applicable rendered gates pass on the exact claimed target; the observed chronology matches the approved story and visual lock; relevant routes, states, viewports, inputs, fallbacks, and utility behavior have direct current evidence; and any required external release was verified on that released/submitted/message surface. Local verification cannot establish deployed fidelity. Screenshots support composition only; they do not prove chronology, input, performance, forms/errors, or route utility.

Completion must state the surface and scope proved, evidence limits, and any remaining issue that is explicitly accepted or outside authority. Do not call an experience finished merely because concept, assets, code, builds, or isolated screenshots look strong.

## 5. Audit authorization without stopping safe work

Treat authority as scoped: direction, continuity source, costly media, visual divergence, local build, and external mutation are separate. Continue reversible in-scope analysis, specification, and local evidence collection. Pause only at the next choice that materially changes the kernel, commits dependent production to an unapproved source, changes a locked presentation, spends on optional media, or creates external state.

For any external action, the approval record must identify the exact destination/account, action/content, spend cap where relevant, recipient/data where relevant, and final-surface verification. Execute only that named action; do not expand to adjacent publishing, purchase, submission, or contact. Once authority is specific, do not manufacture repeated confirmation.

After a gate or authorization rule changes, rerun its direct regression plus an adjacent one. A gate lacking direct evidence is a validation defect, not a pass.
