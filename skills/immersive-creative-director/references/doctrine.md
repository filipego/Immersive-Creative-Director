# Creative doctrine

Use this reference to generate an immersive direction from the subject, visitor, and task. It owns positive creative decisions. `quality-gates.md` owns rejection, sufficiency, and completion judgments; `motion-vocabulary.md` owns mechanism contracts; `production.md` owns media and implementation feasibility.

## Direction sequence

1. Recover the protected kernel and available evidence.
2. Name subject truth and the visitor/business job.
3. Generate three materially different territories from different coherence sources or interpretations.
4. Give each territory a promise, continuity, score, peak, invariant, page-mode behavior, and lowest sufficient scroll tier.
5. Keep two viable territories, recommend one, and describe its few signature scenes in plain language.
6. State the next approval or consequential unknown without assigning implementation labor to the user.

The internal audit holds the reasoning; the user-facing direction leads with the proposed journey and its value.

## `CONCEPT-protected-kernel`

State what must survive: the user's idea, identity, approved visual locks, factual content, audience, business task, desired feeling, and stated preferences. Strengthen the kernel by clarifying relationships, amplifying a latent metaphor or tension, improving sequence, adding a spatial or narrative law, or making conversion belong to the world. A useful direction leaves the originator able to recognize their idea in its stronger form.

## `CONCEPT-subject-truth` and `CONCEPT-visitor-business-job`

Collect concrete inputs before aesthetic language: objects, materials, places, people, gestures, processes, transformations, evidence, data, locations, product states, relationships, tensions, authentic assets, audience expectation, risk, desire, and decision. Translate desired judgments such as “premium” or “cinematic” into observable behavior.

Give the experience a visitor job: understand a space before booking; feel a product transformation; compare proof while retaining narrative pull; navigate a process; assess value; or move from observation to participation. The job determines what immersion is for and where calm utility is more useful than intensity.

## `CONCEPT-coherence-source`

Choose one primary principle that makes varied scenes feel authored together. Select the family the subject can actually sustain:

- **Material law:** one credible material or graphic behavior changes role across type, imagery, thresholds, navigation, and resolution.
- **Productive tension:** a real duality resolves over time through image, type, pace, or public/private modes.
- **Generative metaphor:** an actual business or story relationship supplies several verbs, not just one visual resemblance.
- **Subject relationship:** meaning comes from a persistent relationship among people, product, place, community, or evidence.
- **Route/process:** milestones, dependencies, progress, and arrival organize the experience.
- **Field/system:** topology, density, accumulation, or spatial data expresses multiplicity or scale.
- **Invariant shell:** a stable editorial, camera, or interface grammar gives independent projects room to remain themselves.
- **Ceremonial score:** a repeated chapter ritual holds together meaningful compositional and emotional variation.

One source is primary; supporting ideas serve it. The same direction should become less convincing when transplanted unchanged to an unrelated subject.

## `CONCEPT-experience-promise`

Write one sentence: “The visitor [perceives/does/travels/assembles/discovers] [subject-specific states], so [understanding/feeling/decision] becomes [earned outcome].” The promise governs scene inclusion, proof placement, and the ending.

## `STORY-continuity-type`, `STORY-score`, and `STORY-peak-model`

Choose a dominant continuity type.

- **Causal:** an action or state creates the next; suited to making, transformation, exchange, or consequence.
- **Spatial/editorial:** distance, crop, topology, alignment, field, or a stable shell relates independent material.
- **Rhythmic/ceremonial:** a recurring score, cadence, or refrain creates belonging across chapters.
- **Deliberate hybrid:** handoffs are named and one invariant survives the change.

Build only the score beats the idea needs: threshold/orientation, premise, first proof, variation, escalation or convergence, peak, participation/decision, relief, and resolution. Each beat changes meaning, hierarchy, relationship, evidence, or decision. Select a peak model: **singular**, **cumulative**, **distributed**, or **deliberately quiet**. The choice directs preparation, contrast, and resolution; it is not a mandatory climax template.

## `ORIENT-invariant-frame`

Define a recoverable frame: an object, material law, grid, chapter rail, coordinate system, progress path, navigation surface, typography role, recurring gesture, color function, or camera law. It stays quiet enough to preserve hierarchy while giving visitors a way to reorient as scenes change.

## `PAGE-mode-map`

Assign only relevant modes and vary intensity by task while preserving the shared world.

- **Experience:** atmosphere, participation, and spatial/narrative travel.
- **Proof:** projects, features, data, testimonials, specifications, and results; movement organizes relationships and preserves comparison.
- **Reading:** articles and methodology; movement punctuates, paces, and marks thresholds.
- **Decision:** booking, pricing, applications, configuration, contact, and checkout; controls and consequences stay clear.
- **Utility:** navigation, support, search, filters, forms, legal, and loading/error/empty states; the lowest intensity with complete usability.

For every real route or chapter, map the visitor job, mode, coherence expression, continuity/invariant, focus and density, meaningful state change, proof/decision/utility responsibility, entry/handoff/exit, responsive implication, and final resolution.

## `SCROLL-control-tier`

Choose the lowest tier that expresses the approved score.

- **Native:** intentional flow, sticky utility, bounded transitions.
- **Lightly scrubbed:** progress changes one bounded crop, mask, scale, or path while travel stays physically honest.
- **Authored:** a fixed or pinned stage maps scroll to substantial, oriented scene change with a clear release.
- **Discrete participation:** click, hold, drag, or choice briefly becomes the input because agency has narrative or functional meaning.

For a non-native tier, describe the input ratio, hold, release, reversibility, progress signal, and narrative-parity fallback. Detail a selected behavior in `motion-vocabulary.md` and feasibility in `production.md`.

## Direction record

Retain internally: protected kernel; used and unused subject truths; visitor job; coherence source and alternatives; promise; continuity and handoffs; score and peak; invariant; whole-experience map; scroll tier; likely substrate; transplantation result; and unresolved approval or preference. Use the record to make a direction precise, not to expose framework paperwork.
