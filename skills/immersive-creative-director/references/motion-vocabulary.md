# Motion vocabulary

Use this reference after doctrine establishes the subject-specific coherence source, visitor job, continuity, score, page modes, and scroll tier. It selects conditional mechanisms, not an effects menu. `doctrine.md` owns the premise; `production.md` owns feasibility; `quality-gates.md` decides whether a proposed mechanism is necessary and complete.

Select the smallest family capable of the job: normally one dominant mechanism, one continuity/support mechanism, and one relief/orientation strategy. For each selected ID, record its job, source conditions, assets, input contract, active and suppressed page modes, responsive/fallback behavior, and why the combination belongs only to this subject.

## Selection questions

Before choosing an ID, ask: What subject-specific relationship changes? What must the visitor understand, feel, compare, or cause? What authentic asset or information supports that behavior? What invariant keeps orientation recoverable? Which input—native scroll, bounded scrub, or deliberate participation—best maps to the change? What is its trigger frequency, and therefore its tolerance for duration or ceremony? Must it be interruptible or reversible when input changes? Where does the behavior stop so proof, reading, decision, and utility can work? If native structure already communicates the relationship, preserve it and use editorial relief as the intentional motion decision. When two candidate mechanisms solve the same job, keep the one that makes a materially different visitor understanding possible.

## Mechanisms

### `MOTION-object-state`

Let a meaningful object change condition, role, scale, material, ownership, or meaning. Use when the subject contains lifecycle, making, selection, exchange, growth, assembly, or consequence. Prepare an object/state matrix with start and end meanings, intermediate states, continuity anchors, asset variants, and a fallback sequence. Each interval completes a legible state change. It can pair with material transition, match cut, participation, or camera relationship.

### `MOTION-camera-relationship`

Change apparent distance, angle, framing, or spatial relationship to meaningful media when scale reveals intimacy, construction, evidence, environment, or spatial understanding. Prepare a distance storyboard, crop-safe media, focal anchors, copy-safe regions, and responsive framing variants. Camera change follows narrative distance; the visitor can recover orientation. It pairs well with topology, match cuts, takeovers, and relief.

### `MOTION-topology`

Change composition structure—sheet to stage, pair to field, grid to tunnel, diagram to world—when the story changes how information relates. Define before/after composition maps, invariant anchors, element ownership, responsive variants, and a parity fallback. One topology resolves before the next dominates, and controls remain findable. Combine with material, camera, or a bounded pinned transformation.

### `MOTION-material-transition`

Use a credible physical, graphic, cultural, or procedural material to assemble, dissolve, cut, weave, flood, fragment, reveal, or absorb scenes. Specify the material behavior, source/target ownership, intermediate states, legibility rules, and varied reuse. The material prepares the next scene while resolving the current one. Pair with object state, topology, match cut, or field evolution.

### `MOTION-match-cut`

Hand off through an aligned subject, silhouette, crop, direction, gesture, color, or action when two assets share a real anchor. Provide the paired frames, alignment key, crop coordinates, timing, and responsive alternative. Recognition happens before context changes. Use it for concise continuity, context jumps, and project handoffs.

### `MOTION-path-progression`

Let geometry connect meaningful milestones, evidence, turning points, and arrival. Use for journeys, chronology, process, geography, dependencies, and measured expeditions. Build the milestone/evidence table first, then define each bend or node's semantic role, progress contract, route variants, and static chronology. Path reveal follows actual content progress, retaining offscreen pull without hiding order. It works with an invariant, camera relationship, or one focused hold.

### `MOTION-field-evolution`

Treat particles, points, fragments, crowds, maps, data, or atmosphere as a field whose topology and density change with story state. Use when multiplicity, data, terrain, community, or accumulation is central. Define field job, source geometry, named states, density map, camera/depth contract, legibility voids, performance ceiling, fallback, and destination. Type and media share its spatial logic. Pair with camera, material, topology, or path.

### `MOTION-environmental-takeover`

Let a contained proof or media plane become the surrounding environment at an earned turn, then deliberately release. Use when observation becomes embodiment, invitation, or lived atmosphere. Prepare contained/environmental crops, overlay legibility, entry/hold/release plan, strongest authentic media, and responsive treatment. Stored restraint makes the take-over meaningful; normal flow follows it.

### `MOTION-pinned-transformation`

Hold a composition only while one named internal transformation completes. Use for comparison, assembly, state replacement, spatial explanation, or a concentrated reveal. Define input-to-progress ratio, maximum hold, orientation, release, reversibility, and fallback. Progress stays near-honest except for a short, stated threshold. Pair it with object state, topology, path, or field—not several competing changes.

### `MOTION-participation`

Invite click, hold, drag, pointer, or sound only when action produces a narrative or product-state consequence. Prepare the affordance, expected action, consequence, immediate feedback, cancellation/recovery, keyboard/touch parity, fallback, and explicit audio policy. The action is optional or clearly invited, and its completion/reversal is understandable. It can operate object states, fields, paths, or a bounded transformation.

### `MOTION-editorial-relief`

Lower motion, density, and spatial complexity for reading, comparison, decision, utility, recovery, and contrast. Define information priority, page mode, entry/exit relationship to immersive chapters, and restrained shared invariants. Relief is a complete behavior that prepares the next meaningful contrast.

## Familiar techniques as mechanisms

Admit a text reveal when type behaves as material, evidence, spatial geometry, or orientation. Admit parallax when authored planes share a camera/depth relationship. Use cards for comparison, selection, proof, networking, or utility. Let a floating object have a physical state, depth role, or causal destination. Use shake/pulse as proportional tactile, error, or impact feedback. A particle burst marks an established material/state change. Scrubbed video reveals temporal or spatial evidence. Horizontal travel expresses a genuinely lateral relationship. In every case, record the mechanism ID and contract that gives the technique its job.

## Combination discipline

Name the dominant mechanism and job before support. Each support mechanism solves a different visitor problem. Use an invariant and editorial relief to keep the system readable. A signature move is a subject-specific combination, not a stack of expensive media, WebGL, and interaction. Mechanism selection ends at the behavior contract; implementation specialists realize that approved contract.

## Mechanism record

Keep this compact internal record: dominant ID and job; supporting IDs and distinct jobs; subject-specific source conditions; required assets and continuity risks; input source and trigger frequency; expected response latency versus narrative duration; interruptibility, reversal, and exit behavior; scroll or participation contract; active/suppressed page modes; responsive and fallback counterpart; familiar technique translated to its mechanism; and the subject-specific reason the behavior cannot move unchanged to another brand. Frequent functional actions remain immediate and restrained; ceremonial duration belongs only to infrequent, earned narrative beats. This record provides the handoff to production and quality gates without choosing libraries or choreography. If the approved mechanism becomes continuity-dependent generated motion, translate its state matrix into the mandatory rendered `PREVIS-STATE-*` board and adjacent `PREVIS-EDGE-*` graph in [previsualization.md](previsualization.md); the mechanism record itself is not motion-generation authority.
